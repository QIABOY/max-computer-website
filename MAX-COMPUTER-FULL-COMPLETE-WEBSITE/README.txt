HOSTING STEPS:
1. Replace YOUR-DOMAIN-HERE in index.html, robots.txt and sitemap.xml with your real domain.
2. Replace the Facebook and YouTube placeholder links in index.html with the exact public profile URLs.
3. Upload everything inside this folder to public_html (or the equivalent web root) on your hosting account.
4. Enable SSL/HTTPS.
5. Open your domain and test Home, Services, Products, Google Maps, phone and email buttons.
6. Add/verify the business on Google Business Profile using the same official business name, phone and address.
7. Add the site in Google Search Console and submit sitemap.xml.

The site is static: no PHP, MySQL or Node.js is required to host this version.
